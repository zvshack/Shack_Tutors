About Shack Tutors

This is a central website for connecting students and parents with qualified tutors. Most tutoring companies and programs act as a middle man for tutors. This service ends up costing the parents while the tutors don't end up seeing the profit. Shack Tutors aims to provide a more accessible and affordable solution for both tutors and the parents behind the students while still supplying the best educational support. This webcite is able to cut out the middle man of a company while connecting you to local qualified tutors set at a fraction of the cost.

About Me

My name is Zach Shack and I am a recent graduate from Knox College. I have been tutoring for seven years with a multitude of ages and programs. After graduating, with the job market being tough, I decided to start my own tutoring business to help students and parents find qualified tutors without the high costs associated with traditional tutoring companies. Since there are multiple other tutors who are in a similar position as me, Shack Tutors allows them to connect with more familes and help more students.
